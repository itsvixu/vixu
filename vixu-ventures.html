I'm Vikesh Tailor, the founder of VIXU Ventures — a solo design consultancy based in Stratford, London. With over two decades in the industry, I've led design on some of the most complex and high-stakes digital products in travel, finance, and public sector.

My work spans experience design audits, heuristic evaluations, end-to-end product design, and brand identity — always grounded in deep empathy for users and a sharp eye for what actually works at scale.

Whether it's a greenfield product, a legacy system crying out for clarity, or a brand ready to evolve — I bring strategic thinking and meticulous craft to every engagement.

What I Offer

Services built around
your needs.

Experience Design Audits

A rigorous, evidence-based evaluation of your existing digital product — surfacing friction, inconsistency, and missed opportunities with clear, actionable recommendations.

Heuristic Evaluations

Expert-led usability analysis against established design principles. Fast, cost-effective, and powerful for identifying issues before they reach real users.

Product Design

From concept to high-fidelity prototype, I design digital products that are intuitive, scalable, and genuinely delightful — with a deep focus on user goals and business outcomes.

User Experience Design

End-to-end UX strategy and design: research, journey mapping, information architecture, interaction design, and usability testing — all under one roof.

Brand Identity

Distinctive, considered brand identities that communicate who you are and what you stand for — built to work consistently across digital, print, and beyond.
